Have Questions ?
What kind of real estate projects has SOLD marketed?

SOLD has worked across project launches, ongoing sales campaigns, brokerage marketing, international buyer campaigns and brand-building for property businesses. Our experience spans different price points, audiences and markets, allowing us to adapt the strategy around the commercial objective of each project.

Can SOLD improve an existing campaign that is underperforming?

Yes. We can review the current strategy, targeting, creative, lead quality, website experience and tracking to identify where performance is being lost, then focus on the areas most likely to improve results.

Can SOLD take over campaigns currently managed by another agency?

Yes. SOLD can review your existing campaigns, tracking, creative, targeting and lead quality before taking over management.

The focus is on identifying what is already working, fixing where performance is being lost and improving results without unnecessarily starting from scratch.

Does SOLD have experience marketing luxury and high-value property?

Yes. SOLD has experience marketing premium and high-value real estate to both local and international buyers. This includes campaigns focused on affluent investors, end-users and high-net-worth audiences where positioning, creative quality, targeting and lead qualification are critical.

Does SOLD have experience across residential, commercial and branded real estate?

Yes. SOLD works across residential, commercial and branded real estate, as well as developer and brokerage marketing. Each segment requires a different approach, so we tailor the strategy, messaging and channels around the audience, asset and sales cycle.

What measurable results has SOLD delivered for property clients?

SOLD. has helped property clients achieve results including 95% Phase 1 sell-outs within six weeks, launch-day sell-outs, 25%+ increases in qualified enquiries and sales from international buyers across 20+ countries.

Our focus is always on measurable commercial outcomes more qualified demand, stronger visibility and ultimately more sales.
